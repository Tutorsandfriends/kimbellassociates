Hoarding & Specialty Cleaning

Compassionate, discreet help restoring order and cleanliness to challenging spaces.

Handled with care

A sensitive approach to severe cleaning needs

At Santa Cruz Cleans, we specialize in hoarding cleaning services and severe cleaning needs. We understand that these situations require a unique approach and sensitivity, and we are here to help you restore order and cleanliness to your living spaces.

We offer compassionate and professional services to support individuals and families facing challenging situations — handling every case with sensitivity, respect and efficiency at affordable prices.

How we help

Our process, start to finish

Assessment & Planning

Our experts conduct a thorough assessment and work closely with you to develop a customized cleaning plan.

Clutter Removal

We safely remove clutter, prioritizing important items and disposing of unnecessary items responsibly.

Deep Cleaning

Once cleared, we deep-clean the entire space — sanitizing surfaces, cleaning floors and addressing neglected areas.

Post-Cleanup Support

We help you maintain a clutter-free, organized space and remain available for ongoing cleaning as needed.

Compassionate Approach

We understand the emotional challenges of hoarding, and approach every situation with empathy and compassion.

Discretion & Confidentiality

Your privacy is our priority. Every cleanup is handled with the utmost discretion and strict confidentiality.
