Deep expertise for a policy landscape that never stands still.

Jeffrey J. Kimbell & Associates, Inc. provides strategic legislative, regulatory, policy, and advocacy solutions to clients in the life sciences community. The firm combines deep policy expertise with a comprehensive suite of communications and public affairs services.

Founded in 1998, Kimbell & Associates has grown to include 25 full-time professionals, including 11 lobbyists and 7 healthcare policy experts. Today, the firm represents more than 85 clients, primarily across the life sciences industry.

Drawing on experience in both the public and private sectors, the firm’s principals bring extensive institutional knowledge and relationships developed through service to Members of Congress, Governors, federal agencies, and private-sector organizations.

That integrated perspective helps clients identify opportunities, navigate challenges, engage key stakeholders, and advance their interests before Congress, the Administration, regulatory agencies, and the broader healthcare and technology communities.
